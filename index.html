🍽️ Food & Life

From the Kitchen • Quick & Easy

Ginger-Lime Chicken & Rice Bowls

Serves 2 • About 25 minutes

You’ll need: 8 oz boneless skinless chicken breast, 1 cup cooked rice, 1 cucumber, 1 carrot, 1 tablespoon garlic-infused olive oil (optional), 1 teaspoon grated ginger, 1 lime, and pepper.

Slice the chicken into thin strips and cook in the oil with ginger and pepper until it reaches 165°F. Divide the rice between two bowls, add sliced cucumber and grated carrot, then top with chicken and a squeeze of lime.

Easy swap: use a smaller rice portion and extra vegetables. Skip any ingredient that doesn’t agree with you.

⚡ Jay's Corner: Survival Gear & Preparedness

Preparedness • Gear reality check

Jackery Explorer 300 Plus: small battery, sensible expectations

Illustration only · not a product photograph

Gear desk (specifications previously checked): a compact backup power station with a 288 Wh battery, 300 W rated AC output, 600 W surge capability and a weight of about 8.27 lb. Useful for phones, lights and some small electronics—provided their actual power needs fit the limits.

The verdict: Handy during an outage; not a substitute for a whole-house generator. In an apocalypse, it's a good idea to know what your gear can do before the lights go out. Jay would probably be the calm one reading the manual while Felicia asks why the universe has terrible timing.

Farm/rain warning: Jackery explicitly says the Explorer 300 Plus does not support waterproof or dustproof protection. Keep it dry and protected. It is not a suitable exposed-to-rain solution for Jay’s farm.
